# THD Simulations
Another possible feature I considered adding was modifications to the diode wave shaper network (more diodes, more resistors, etc.)
To validate the output waveform shape, I conducted a .four FFT simulation with THD analysis in LTSpice.
I measured the frequency of the waveform to be 2.045kHz, with a initial delay of 0.475ms, so I set up the fourier analysis like so: 
<br><br>
<img width="351" height="81" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/8ef4d5f1-abaf-4cfd-bb40-f3b9246541b5" />
<br><br>
Initial THD reads came back inflated (~56%) despite a visually clean waveform. The root cause was I used the theoretical frequency based off of TR1's resistance for .four's frequency argument, which did not match the oscillator's actual measured frequency. I solved this by measuring 10 periods of the waveform and dividing the time elapsed by 10 to find period and frequency.
<br><br>
<img width="1917" height="873" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1afa46a4-2959-4357-a404-f16c61a1d207" />
<br><br>
The THD analysis yielded a sufficiently low distortion percentage of 3.251%, meaning no additional modifications to the diode wave shaper network are necessary. For reference, the triangle wave output yields a THD reading of ~11%.
