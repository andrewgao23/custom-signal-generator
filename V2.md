Added features in V2:
Output waveform is now centered at 2.5V with a 5V pp voltage (alternating from 0V to 5V). This change was made as some loads did not deal well with negative voltage inputs.
Schematic now reflects the +8V and -8V main power rails that were used for simulation in LTSpice. (+5V and -5V power rails were not enough for op amp biases and output voltage was clipped.)

Added an analog sum sub-circuit that biases a DC voltage onto the output.
